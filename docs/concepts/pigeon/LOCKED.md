# LOCKED — Level B pigeon ornithopter

**Project title:** Pigeon ornithopter (Level B)  
**Decision date:** 2026-08-10  
**Status:** locked success bar (not built yet)  
**Path:** `docs/concepts/pigeon/`

## What we are building

An **anatomically inspired rock-pigeon ornithopter** for outdoor use at distance:

- Mass / span in rock-pigeon range (~300–500 g, ~60–70 cm span)  
- Camera aboard; view on goggles and/or iPhone  
- **Primary remote: radio handset** (ExpressLRS-class); iPhone is viewer/map only  
- Anatomical accuracy **Level B** (see below)  

Gram-scale insect flyers and Level C museum finish are **out of scope** unless this lock is explicitly reopened.

## Level B means (acceptance checklist)

Must all be true:

- [ ] Wing planform matches pigeon (primary/secondary layout, not a bat triangle)  
- [ ] Shoulder–elbow–wrist motion inspired by real joint coupling (under-actuated OK if the *path* looks avian)  
- [ ] Wing surface is feathered or feather-analogue and changes area / overlaps in flight  
- [ ] Active wing twist (or proven passive twist that works) — not flat paddle flapping only  
- [ ] Tail used for pitch/yaw like a bird  
- [ ] Legs fold in flight and can stand/perch (clumsy OK)  
- [ ] Head–body–tail proportions read as pigeon at park distance  
- [ ] Camera integrated in head or breast — no dangling brick  
- [ ] Outdoor controlled flight on a mild day from a handset  
- [ ] Usable camera view at park-scale distance  

## Explicitly not required for “done”

- Birder-uncertain at 5 m (that’s Level C)  
- Head-bob animation, breathing belly, perfect iridescence  
- iPhone-only control  
- Hover like a quad  
- Covert / “spy wildlife” use case
