**Language note:** older drafts said “living room product.” That only meant *indoors at home, something you can actually use* — not a store shelf, not a special product category.

**Verdict:** The ask is doable **if** we stop copying phone-drone architecture downward and instead invent an airframe that only carries what physics requires: an aperture, a transducer, a modulator, actuators, and a thin energy store — while the iPhone (plus a palm-sized optical companion) carries energy delivery, interrogation light, compute, and the human interface.

Historical “impossible at 1–3 g” conclusions mostly measured the wrong object: a shrunk quad with a CMOS module and a Wi‑Fi SoC. That object is heavy. **It is not a law of nature.**
