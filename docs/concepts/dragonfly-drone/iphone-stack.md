# iPhone — viewer and mission computer (not the outdoor remote)

Superseded as **primary outdoor control** by [`remote-control.md`](remote-control.md) and [`field-revision.md`](field-revision.md).

## What the phone should do

- Show camera / Pinna-reconstructed video (fed from a ground receiver)  
- Show telemetry (battery, RSSI, mode) when bridged from the radio or craft  
- Mission notes, logging, clip review  
- Optional clamp on the handset  

## What the phone should not do outdoors

- Be the only stick input  
- Be the only long-range RF path (SoftAP / BLE as primary)  

## Indoor / bench

Phone SoftAP sticks are still fine for desk bring-up and Phase A display. See [`phase-a-first-weekend.md`](phase-a-first-weekend.md).
