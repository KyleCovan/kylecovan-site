Something that tripped me up this week, in case it's tripping anyone else up.

In Cursor, the word "cloud" means two different things.

One is a brain. The thinking happens on their servers. It has no folder and no terminal and no copy of your project. It just reasons.

The other is a machine. A temporary Linux computer they spin up to actually do things: clone the repo, run commands, open a browser. Hands.

Same brain either way. The only choice you're making is whose hands it uses, yours or theirs. Yours means your laptop has to be awake. Theirs means it doesn't.

Was going to be a whole troubleshooting session. Turned out to be a vocabulary problem.
