I wrote a sentence down that I have not been able to leave alone:

What consciousness discovers tends to glory as its own discovery.

Here is what I mean.

A person finds something. He understands it inside the limits of what he can see. He validates it against his own perception. Then he holds it up as a high achievement, and because he found it, he glories in it as if the finding made it his.

The conscious mind is very good at this. It confuses the thing discovered with the source of the thing. It promotes what it has grasped, often with real ignorance of where that grasp came from, and of how little of the whole it actually holds. Sometimes it is only holding up its own limitation, and calling that limitation wisdom.

There is so much more outside that small circle. Wisdom, love, knowledge, understanding, invention, creation. Depths that make our proudest findings look trivial. And still we can live as if the circle we mapped is the map.

The danger is not curiosity. It is the glorying. Discovery becomes a mirror. The mirror becomes the point. The source is forgotten. We get consumed with ourselves, pursuing our own devices day to day, and that road does not lead where we think.

I am not saying this about "people." I am saying it because I recognize the pattern in myself: the satisfaction of having figured something out, and the quiet promotion of that figuring.

The corrective is not to stop discovering. It is to stop mistaking the discovery for the source, and a limited grasp for the depth that remains.
