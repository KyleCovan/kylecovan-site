Tapo Canyon sells one thing: I read your website and tell you what on it is no longer true. Hours that changed. A staff member who left. A number that stopped working.

Tonight I found three different answers on my own site to the question of what the free report actually includes.

The hero said it comes with the fix. The How I Work section said it was a list of what's wrong and stopped there. The FAQ said that if something turned up, we could talk about what to do next, which reads like the fix is a separate conversation you have to ask for.

Then I opened the actual report pages I send people. They say the findings come "with the wording to fix them." So the product does include the fix. Two of the three descriptions were selling it short, and one of them was quietly telling people to expect less than they'd get.

None of this was a lie. Each sentence was written on a different day, for a different spot on the page, and each one was true enough when I wrote it. That's the whole problem. Nobody sits down and decides to contradict themselves. You describe the same thing in three places over a few weeks, and the three descriptions drift, and no single edit is where it went wrong.

I found the same thing an hour later in a different file. I keep a set of written instructions for building the report pages. One section says never call those pages private, because "not indexed" and "not private" are different things and I can't promise the second one. Further down, in the short list of hard rules, it said the pages are private links.

The correct rule and the wrong one were in the same file, about four hundred words apart. And the wrong one was in the section you'd skim to if you were in a hurry.

So the lesson isn't really about websites. Anywhere you say the same thing in more than one place, the copies will eventually disagree. That's not a discipline problem, it's just what happens. The question is whether you ever go back and read them side by side.

Which is, word for word, the service I sell. I check other people's pages against each other and tell them where they disagree. I had not done it to my own.

I fixed all four spots tonight. What I don't have is a way to catch the next one, and I'm not sure a rule would help, since the rule I already had is the thing that contradicted itself.
