**Thread**

1/
I had a rule: keep one offer off the homepage.

Offer it only when a free report found mismatched facts across Google and directories.

One outreach door.

2/
Then I audited the live page.

The offer was already named. Twice.

Tease. FAQ.

A visitor could see the name and still not know what they were buying.

3/
Halfway naming was worse than a real card.

So I overrode myself. The offer is explained on the page now.

Still no second call to action. The free report remains the only door.

4/
Lesson:

If the page already says the word, finish the sentence or take the word off.
