I had a rule: keep the Consistency Sweep off the homepage. Offer it only when a free mini report found mismatched facts across directories. One outreach door.

Then I audited the live page.

The Sweep was already named. Twice. Tease and FAQ. A visitor could see the name and still not know what they were buying.

Halfway naming was worse than a real card.

So I overrode myself. The Sweep is on the page now. It explains the work. It still has no separate call to action. The free mini report remains the only door.

Lesson: if the page already says the word, finish the sentence or take the word off.
