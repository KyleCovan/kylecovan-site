I ranked what I can actually sell today. Not a catalog brainstorm. A hard look at strengths that map to jobs a stranger will pay for without a long trust runway.

Two came through.

First: quiet website rot. Hours wrong, dead buttons, staff pages that lie. A monthly accuracy pass that finds the drift, fixes the top issues, and sends a short report. Door in front of it: a free mini rot report on a real public site.

Second: when AI tools make a mess. Wrong promises, bad summaries, a trail nobody can read. Rebuild the timeline. Write a plain memo. Flat fee. Not legal advice. That one waits for a bad day. It is not the weekly door.

They are not competing products. They are rungs on one ladder, with the heavier client AI OS work kept off the public site for now.

Ranking was useful when it forced the strength question. It is a distraction when it invents a third offer.
