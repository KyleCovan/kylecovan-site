Quick share from a ranking session that actually clarified the offer instead of expanding it.

I asked which of my strengths map to a job a stranger would pay for without a long trust runway. Two answers:

- A monthly accuracy pass on public websites (quiet rot: wrong hours, dead links, stale staff pages), with a free mini rot report as the door
- AI incident reconstruction when tools create a mess: timeline + plain-English memo, flat fee, not legal advice, triggered demand

The useful move was treating them as rungs on one ladder instead of competing products. The heavier client AI OS work stays off the public site for now.

If you've used a ranker or scorecard on your own offers: did it shrink the pile or grow it?
