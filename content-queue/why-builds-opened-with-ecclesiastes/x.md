For a while my builds page opened with Ecclesiastes 11:1, above the heading, not as it.

Cast your bread upon the waters. A build log is that: send the unfinished work out and trust you'll find it after many days.

I almost named the page "Upon the Waters." It would have sat next to "Unless the Lord" in the nav. Two Scripture names like that cancel each other out, so the verse carried the phrase and the title stayed unspent.

One writing door now. Ecclesiastes left with the old builds page. Psalm 127 stayed. Deliberately.
