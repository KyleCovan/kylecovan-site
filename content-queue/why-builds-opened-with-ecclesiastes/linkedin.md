For a while, the page where I kept what I was building opened with Ecclesiastes 11:1: cast your bread upon the waters, for you will find it after many days.

It sat above the heading, never as the heading. Scripture for the person who arrived. A plain title for search engines and screen readers.

That verse fit a build log. You send the work out unfinished, say what broke, and you do not know what comes back. A portfolio is a finished claim. A log is bread on the water.

I almost named the page "Upon the Waters." It would have sat in the nav next to "Unless the Lord." Two Scripture names like that stop working as names, so I quoted the verse instead and held the title for later.

The site now has one writing door. When the builds page stopped being a door, that verse went with it. Psalm 127 stayed on Unless the Lord. I did not move Ecclesiastes onto the same page.

The bread is still being cast. It just lives in one house now.
