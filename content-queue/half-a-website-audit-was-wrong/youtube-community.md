Someone sent me a 20 page audit of my site, ending with an offer to fix it all for $1,500.

I checked it before touching anything. About half of it was wrong.

Its top critical finding was that my sitemap is broken. It isn't. It works fine. The scanner had looked for it in seven places, six of which don't exist on my site, and my site was handing back the homepage with a success code for every one of them. Six confusing answers, one wrong conclusion.

The tool didn't lie. It reported exactly what it measured. What it measured wasn't true.

Two findings were real though, and one of them I'd never have caught myself: my contact link was dead for every search engine and AI assistant, because of a privacy feature that turned out to be protecting nothing.

Full write up is on the site.

Question for you: have you ever "fixed" something based on a tool's report, only to find out later it was never broken?
