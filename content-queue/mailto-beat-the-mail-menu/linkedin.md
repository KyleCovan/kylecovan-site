I built a polite mail menu on the free report button. Gmail. Outlook on the web. The mail app. Let people choose.

Live testing killed it.

Web compose was flaky. Sometimes it opened the wrong kind of tab. Sometimes it looked like the site was broken. The owners I'm writing for mostly live in the mail app anyway.

Back to plain mailto. Subject and body filled in. One click.

A choice that looks considerate can be friction when the default already works.
