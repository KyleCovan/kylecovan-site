Most of my homepage is about websites. Then two things about AI show up with no warning.

I asked for a divider. One line to smooth the transition.

Attempt one: "for specific situations." Reasonable. Needed work.
Attempt two: a before-and-after framing. Closer. Not right.
Attempt three: rearranged, still off.

Each one slightly better. None of them fixed it. I couldn't say why.

Here's why. Every version was labeling how often you'd buy the thing. Ongoing versus one-time.

But that was never the problem. The problem was that a reader had been reading about websites for the whole page and suddenly hit AI. That's a subject problem. No label about timing was going to fix it, no matter how well written.

I'd been asking for a better sentence when I should have been asking what the sentence was for.

Once that was clear, one pass did it. Give the AI items their own section with a heading that names the subject, so you see the change coming.

Then I found the same mistake one level up. The first group had a label that just said "Services." Fine alone. But sitting next to a label about AI, it quietly told the reader that the AI things weren't services.

Two labels next to each other make a claim about each other. I'd never have caught that looking at either one by itself.

The tell worth keeping: when a sentence keeps almost working, and every rewrite is a little better and still wrong, the sentence usually isn't the problem. It's answering a question nobody asked.
