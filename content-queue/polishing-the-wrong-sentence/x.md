**Thread**

1/
Most of my homepage is about websites.

Then two AI services show up with no warning.

I asked for a divider. One line to smooth the jump.

2/
Attempt 1: "for specific situations." Reasonable. Needed work.

Attempt 2: a before-and-after framing. Closer. Not right.

Attempt 3: rearranged. Still off.

Each one better. None of them fixed it.

3/
Here's why.

Every version labeled how often you'd buy the thing. Ongoing vs one-time.

But that was never the problem.

4/
The problem was that a reader spent the whole page on websites and then hit AI with no setup.

That's a subject problem.

No label about timing fixes it. However well written.

5/
I was asking for a better sentence.

I should have been asking what the sentence was for.

6/
Once that was clear it took one pass:

give the AI items their own section, with a heading that names the subject.

7/
Then I found the same mistake one level up.

The first group's label just said "Services."

Fine alone. But sitting next to "Using AI at work," it quietly said the AI things weren't services.

8/
Two labels next to each other make a claim about each other.

Wouldn't have caught it looking at either one alone.

9/
The tell:

when a sentence keeps almost working, and every rewrite is a bit better and still wrong, the sentence isn't the problem.

It's answering a question nobody asked.
