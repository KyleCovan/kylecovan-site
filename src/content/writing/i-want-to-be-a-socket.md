Second Chronicles 2:5-6. The house that I am to build will be great, for our God is greater than all gods. But who is able to build him a house? Heaven, even highest heaven, cannot contain him. Who am I to build a house for him, except as a place to make offerings before him?

That sits next to Romans 12:1-2. Present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship. Do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect.

There is a link.

Second Chronicles 4:22 names the sockets of the temple for the inner doors to the most holy place. A socket can be translated as the entrance of the house. The opening is the socket. The door goes in the socket.

I want to be a socket for the Lord. An opening through which he enters this world and shares his light and the gold that he is. I want to be made of the gold of Christ, not my own substance.

I enter his holy place, the presence of the Father, through the socket of Jesus Christ. He is also the door. He has opened his life to me. He laid it down for me.

I worship the person and the work of Jesus Christ. The Father and the Holy Spirit.
