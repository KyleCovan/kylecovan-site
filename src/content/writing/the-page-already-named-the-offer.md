I had a rule. The Consistency Sweep was not a homepage product yet. It lived inside the freebie motion: if a mini report found mismatched hours across directories, offer the paid sweep then. Do not invent a second front door.

Then I audited the live page.

The Sweep was already named. Twice. Once in a tease under the monthly plan, once in the FAQ. A visitor could read the name and still not know what they were buying. That halfway state was worse than putting the offer on the page.

So I overrode the earlier ruling. The Sweep is a card now. It sits after the monthly accuracy pass and before the website build. It explains the work. It does not get its own call to action. The free mini report remains the only outreach door. The public price stays "priced by scope" until real quotes exist. Staff Change Protocol Pack and Appointment Leak Simulator stay where they were: freebie and shelf, not new homepage rungs.

The lesson was not "add more products." It was that naming something without explaining it is a kind of noise. If the page already says the word, finish the sentence or take the word off.
