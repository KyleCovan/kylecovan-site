My years in startup operations gave me an understanding of how work actually
moves through a business, and where it gets stuck. I look for the bottlenecks
first, then automate only where it earns its place. Done well, a business gets
more out of what it already has, and the people doing the work become managers
of agents rather than the ones repeating the steps.

Building and deploying a system is only half the work. A tool does no good
unless people actually use it, so I care as much about hands-on training and
steady check-ins as I do about the build itself.
