What business should I make.

That was the question I kept asking. Former paychecks. Hours clocked for an employer. I looked at the scope too narrowly.

I am a thinker. A ponderer. A meditator, founded on Jesus Christ. I pray. I have a desire to share Him. To share thoughts. To share the voice of Him through the socket He made, uniquely.

I have experience thinking. Pondering. Searching for truth. Having found the truth, namely Jesus Christ. Reflecting on Him in the events of my day, and in this age, by sharing the truth.

Call it preaching. Evangelizing. Defending. Living. Working. Speaking. Teaching. Missionary. Worker. Builder. Disciple. Mentor. Counselor. Friend. Brother. They all share. What they share is Jesus Christ.

There is no need to muzzle this ox while I am threshing the grain. There is no shame in Him providing for me as I do these things for His name.

The tools of this age are here. Social. The website. The ports of the internet. AI, organized. Face to face, day to day. Digital and physical as one mission. For His glory, not mine.

God blesses me with ideas. Thoughts of Him. Lessons of Him. Creative articulations of Him. Love for Him. That is where the experience is. I am seeing it at the core now.
