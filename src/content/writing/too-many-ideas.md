---
title: "Too many ideas, and no way to rank them"
date: 2026-07-28
build: personal-ai-os
---

I tend to have a lot of ideas. I'd start a project, then another idea would pull
me sideways, and the first one would sit there unfinished. What I needed wasn't
more ideas. I needed somewhere to put them, and something to tell me which ones
were worth my time.

So it keeps track of all of them and gives them a ranking. That shows me which
ones to focus on first, which ones compound, and which ones aren't worth putting
time or energy into. The point is to keep me structured enough to focus
deliberately instead of getting sidetracked by whatever idea came last.

Today it watches my calendar, my Gmail, and my Obsidian vault, which is where my
notes and random thoughts end up. It's connected to OBS, so it records for me.
And it takes a rough idea and fleshes it out into structured steps in a roadmap,
so I can see what's next and how to go about it.

Here is the thing it catches that I couldn't. I'll have the same idea on
different days, worded differently each time, and not notice. It sees that
they're the same thing and puts them together.

Next is a pipeline for making YouTube videos and social content in a streamlined
way, so most of that work comes off my plate.
