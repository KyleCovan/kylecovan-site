I finished 1 Chronicles. A few things sat with me.

Yahweh is a God of order. He spoke everything into existence and it has order. David was orderly. Organized. He followed the commandments God gave Moses, which were specific and precise.

I want to be an orderly servant of Yahweh. Praising his name. Not forsaking order. Not embracing disorder. Not glorying in it. I want to stand in an orderly fashion for Christ Jesus.

I also saw the free will offering. In addition to the tithe, there are gifts from a glad and worshipful heart. Give as much as you want to God. It is already his. Everything we have is his. We offer it freely back.

David did not only hand Solomon a seat and a pile of wealth. He handed him a blueprint of work. A charge. A command. A purpose. Part of a gift is the gift of work. Not busy work. Work that magnifies the Lord.

An inheritance has many parts. Wealth. Purpose. Blueprint. Charge. Command. To pass on only the wealth and leave out the rest is often, maybe always, a disservice to the heirs.
